📖 Garden Guide

Getting Started

Welcome to Grow a Garden! Plant seeds, water them, watch them grow, and harvest fruits to sell for Sheckles.

Controls

  • 1-7 / Click Toolbar: Select tool
  • Left Click: Use tool on garden
  • Right Click: Favorite item / Cancel
  • Mouse Wheel: Zoom camera
  • Right Drag / WASD: Move camera
  • C: Collect All harvestable
  • G: Grow All (costs Robux)

Mutations

Mutations multiply crop value. They stack additively: Multiplier = 1 + Σ(mutation_mult - 1). Weather, pets, sprinklers, and fertilizers can apply mutations.

Pets

Hatch eggs to get pets. Each pet has a passive ability (mutation, growth speed, harvest bonuses, etc.). Equip up to 3 pets (expandable). Pets level up over time.

Progression

Sell crops → Earn Sheckles → Buy better seeds/gear → Expand plot → Ascend for Garden Coins → Permanent upgrades.

Weather Events

Server-wide weather changes periodically. Each weather boosts specific mutations and growth speed. Check the weather display!
